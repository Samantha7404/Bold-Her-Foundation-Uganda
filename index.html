Our Story

A movement born from purpose

BoldHer Foundation Uganda is a non-profit organisation dedicated to the advancement of women and girls across Uganda. We believe every woman deserves the tools, knowledge, and confidence to shape her own destiny.

Founded on the conviction that empowered women build stronger families and communities, we work at the intersection of education, economic independence, and personal development — creating pathways to transformation that last a lifetime.

We are more than a foundation. We are a movement. A sisterhood. A promise that no woman stands alone on her journey to greatness.

Three Pillars

Everything we do is built
on these foundations

01

Empower

We equip women and girls with the skills, resources, and self-belief to take charge of their lives. Through mentorship, leadership training, and entrepreneurship support, we create confident leaders ready to inspire change.

02

Educate

Education is the most powerful weapon for change. We provide access to quality learning, vocational training, digital literacy, and scholarships — breaking barriers that have historically kept girls out of school.

03

Inspire

Every woman who rises lifts others. We celebrate stories of resilience, create platforms for women's voices to be heard, and build a community of hope — inspiring the next generation to dream bigger.

Our Programs

Initiatives driving real change

Our programs are designed with women and girls at the centre — meeting them where they are, and walking with them toward where they want to be.

Leadership

BoldHer Leadership Academy

An intensive mentorship and leadership program developing the next generation of bold female leaders in Uganda — from grassroots community advocates to business entrepreneurs.

Education

Girls Back to School Initiative

Providing scholarships, school supplies, and support systems to keep vulnerable girls in school and equip them with the education they deserve, regardless of circumstance.

Economic

Women Entrepreneurship Hub

Supporting women-owned small businesses through training, seed funding, market linkages, and mentorship — building economic independence from the ground up.

Wellness

Mental Health & Wellness Circles

Safe spaces for women and girls to talk, heal, and grow together. Community-based mental health support, counselling access, and wellness workshops across Uganda.

Our Vision & Values

A Uganda where every woman thrives

We envision a Uganda where no woman is held back by circumstance, culture, or lack of opportunity — where every girl grows up knowing her potential is limitless.

Our work is guided by unwavering values that shape every programme, every partnership, and every interaction we have.

  • Dignity — every woman deserves respect and recognition
  • Boldness — we champion courage over fear
  • Community — we are stronger together than apart
  • Inclusivity — no woman is left behind
  • Integrity — transparent, accountable, and purpose-driven
