Our Story

A movement born from purpose

BoldHer Foundation Uganda is a non-profit organisation dedicated to the advancement of girls and youth across Uganda. We believe every young person deserves the tools, knowledge and confidence to take control of their future.

Founded on March 3rd, 2025, by a dedicated team of social change makers, we work at the intersection of menstrual health awareness, education, leadership development, and economic empowerment — creating pathways for sustainable transformation.

We are more than a foundation. We are a movement. A sisterhood. A promise that no girl or young person stands alone on their journey to greatness.

Our Goals

Five key objectives

Menstrual Health Awareness

Educate girls and boys about menstruation, reduce stigma, and improve menstrual hygiene management in schools and communities.

Reduce School Dropout

Support girls with knowledge, mentorship, and practical solutions like reusable sanitary pads to keep them in school confidently.

Income-Generating Skills

Train young people in soap making, paper bag production, brick making, and sack farming for self-reliance and poverty reduction.

Youth Economic Empowerment

Help young people earn income that supports their education, personal needs, and family wellbeing for sustainable development.

Community & Youth Leadership

Mobilize volunteers, schools, parents, and communities to develop responsible future leaders creating positive change in Uganda and Africa.

Core Foundations

Built on three pillars

01

Empower

We equip girls and youth with skills, resources and self-belief to take charge of their lives through mentorship, leadership training and entrepreneurship support.

02

Educate

Education is the most powerful weapon for change. We provide quality learning, vocational training, digital literacy and scholarships — breaking historical barriers.

03

Inspire

Every woman and girl who rises lifts others. We celebrate resilience, create platforms for voices and build a community of hope for future generations.

Our Values

Principles that guide us

Empowerment

We believe every girl and young person deserves opportunities to achieve their full potential through education, leadership and life skills.

Equality

We promote fairness, inclusion, dignity and respect by creating equal opportunities and reducing discrimination and stigma in communities.

Integrity

We work with honesty, accountability and transparency in all programs and partnerships to build trust with communities and stakeholders.

Compassion

We care deeply about the wellbeing and future of vulnerable children and communities by providing practical, sustainable support with humanity.

Innovation

We encourage creative, affordable and locally-driven solutions using practical skills, entrepreneurship and sustainable approaches.

Teamwork

We believe strong partnerships, volunteerism and cooperation are essential because transforming Uganda requires collective responsibility and unity.

Our Programs

Initiatives driving real change

Our programs are designed with girls and youth at the centre, meeting them where they are and walking with them toward where they want to be.

Leadership

Leadership Academy

An intensive mentorship program developing the next generation of bold female leaders in Uganda — from community advocates to business entrepreneurs.

Education

Back to School Initiative

Scholarships, school supplies and support systems to keep vulnerable girls in school and equip them with the education they deserve.

Economic

Entrepreneurship Hub

Supporting youth-led businesses through training, seed funding, market linkages and mentorship — building economic independence.

Health

Menstrual Health Program

Awareness, hygiene education, and distribution of reusable sanitary pads to ensure girls' health and educational continuity.

Our Vision

An empowered Africa where every girl thrives

We envision an Africa where no girl or young person is held back by circumstance, culture, or lack of opportunity. Where every young person is educated, healthy, skilled, confident, economically independent, and given equal opportunities to achieve their full potential.

Our commitment is to breaking barriers caused by poverty, menstrual stigma and inequality by empowering girls and youth through practical, life-changing interventions.

  • Dignity — every person deserves respect and recognition
  • Boldness — we champion courage and confidence
  • Community — we are stronger together than apart
  • Inclusivity — no one is left behind
  • Integrity — transparent and purpose-driven impact
